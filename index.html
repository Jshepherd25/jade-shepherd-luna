About

Hi, I'm Jade — at heart, a creative entrepreneur who thrives at the intersection of art, storytelling, and conservation.

Through my studio, The Perpetual Pen, I craft ink-based fine art that merges nature with enchantment, building a brand world where every piece feels like both a relic and a story. I don't just make art — I manage the full experience: designing immersive packaging, running launches, writing lore, and cultivating a community of “Guardians” who join me in supporting wildlife conservation.

Alongside that, I'm a budding programmer. I'm currently studying JavaScript, HTML, and CSS, and diving into projects that stretch both my logic and imagination. From crafting small interactive games to exploring creative coding with JavaScript, I see programming as another tool for storytelling — a way to build digital spaces that spark wonder and connection, just as my artwork does in the physical world.

By day, I also work as a leasing professional, where I collaborate with teams to create welcoming spaces for residents. I plan community events, coordinate vendors, and guide people through the process of finding their new homes. Property management has sharpened my communication and organizational skills, taught me the value of empathy in customer service, and given me a strong foundation in balancing business operations with people-first care.

Altogether, my path blends artistry, technology, and community-building — and I'm excited to keep growing at the crossroads of all three.
